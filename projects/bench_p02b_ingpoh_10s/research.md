## Pertanyaan Riset Utama
1. Bagaimana AC mendinginkan ruangan tertutup tanpa menyedot udara dingin dari luar?
2. Bagaimana kumparan evaporator dan unit kondensor luar memindahkan kalor ruangan?

## Klaim Faktual Terverifikasi & Kutipan Bukti
- **`CLM_01` [verified_fact]**: Evaporator AC indoor menyerap kalor udara ruangan ketika refrigeran cair bertekanan rendah menguap menjadi gas di dalam kumparan pipa.
  - Sumber: *U.S. Department of Energy — Energy Saver: Air Conditioning* (2024-06) — https://www.energy.gov/energysaver/air-conditioning
  - Ringkasan Bukti: Air conditioners transfer heat from the indoor air to the outdoors via an indoor evaporator coil filled with cold evaporating refrigerant.
- **`CLM_02` [verified_fact]**: Kompresor menaikkan tekanan dan suhu uap refrigeran sehingga sirip kondensor outdoor dapat membuang kalor ke udara luar.
  - Sumber: *Encyclopaedia Britannica — Air-conditioning: Principles of Operation* (2024-08) — https://www.britannica.com/technology/air-conditioning
  - Ringkasan Bukti: In vapor-compression air conditioning, the compressor raises refrigerant vapor pressure and temperature so the outdoor condenser coil rejects heat to ambient air.
- **`CLM_03` [verified_fact]**: Termostat AC menjaga suhu ruangan nyaman stabil pada setelan target seperti 24 derajat Celsius tanpa mempercepat laju pendinginan awal saat disetel ekstrem rendah.
  - Sumber: *U.S. Department of Energy — Central Air Conditioning* (2024-05) — https://www.energy.gov/energysaver/central-air-conditioning
  - Ringkasan Bukti: Setting the thermostat lower than comfortable target temperature does not cool the room faster; the system removes heat at a steady rate.
